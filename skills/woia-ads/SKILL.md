# Ads orchestration

Read [the methodology](references/methodology.md) and [the manifest](../../dev.woia/manifest.json). Select one generic Ads root per organization/Project. Require qualified immutable WOIA Core >=0.5.3 binding.

1. Resolve accepted objective, context, account/resource/destination, timing, budget/currency and competent authority; do not invent missing values or underlying business terms.
2. Prepare plan/assets/measurement through shared Strategy, Audience, Copy and Creative. Access suitable resources directly; request Marketing only for distinct missing outcomes. Ads owns paid variants/tests and conversion measurement, originating owners own business facts.
3. Use [eligibility helper](scripts/coordinate.mjs) against trusted accepted policy; its proposal does not execute or grant authority. Qualified Ads Platforms performs exact paid effects with idempotency. Reconcile unknown submitted outcomes before retry; do not expand limits.
4. Measure source/period/population/definition/freshness/coverage and attribution limits. Missing data is UNKNOWN, not zero. Conversion is not cash, conversation is not qualification, booking is not attendance.
5. Improve, pause, escalate or close inside authority. Preserve unknown effects/billing work, owner, evidence, rationale and next review through Core Tasks/Due Work; do not add a resident worker/scheduler.

Human inquiry/message/comment becomes normalized inbound Interaction via Communications to Customer Service. Ads never answers people or mutates appointments. Finance owns accepted Ledger/Payments consequences; Technology access/bindings; Data source integrity; Software engineering. Generic Ads consumes supplied offering references without copying Property/Mandate/Lease logic or an industry delta.

No live adapter, Operator E2E or Production Ready is claimed.
