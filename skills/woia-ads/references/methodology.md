# Generic Ads methodology contract


Five phases: objective/context/scope; plan/assets/measurement; authorized effects; delivery/signal/results; improvement/pause/escalation/closure. This is methodology, not an authority service or a master of business facts. Source Authority Map identifies competent owners and accepted revisions. Evidence/extraction/inference never substitutes accepted fact.

Core minimum0.5.6 resolves through immutable qualified Project binding. Capabilities retain implementation; installation or technical access does not authorize execution. Trusted policy is independently accepted by competent owner and verified by host; the helper does not authenticate caller or authority. Policy defines actual account, action, resource, destination, currency, remaining minor units and approver. No invented budget, universal hard-cap or provider guarantee. Pure eligibility is not concurrency-safe spend reservation: qualified platform controls must enforce actual limits.

A prior UNKNOWN result retains idempotency/source identity and reconciles before retry. A successful/pending prior effect cannot be dispatched again. Reconciliation does not grant broader authority. Closure preserves unresolved effects/billing and receiver-owned continuation.

Ads owns paid targeting/budget/conversion/campaign outcomes and paid measurement. Marketing owns brand/non-paid outcomes. Human interactions preserve event/source/correlation/attribution and route via Communications to Customer Service, never person-directed dispatch here. Scheduling and external contact are unavailable. Finance owns accounting/cash acceptance, Data is not a universal writer. No autonomous negotiation, financial write or authority expansion.

Measurement retains sources, periods, populations, definitions, deterministic calculations, freshness, coverage and attribution uncertainty. Missing measurement remains UNKNOWN. Business result acceptance remains with the competent owner. Technology handles credentials/connectivity; Software actual code changes; organizational policy defines intervals/thresholds, not this generic method.

The exact effect proposal is bound to a current, non-revoked accepted grant matching organization, actor, purpose, source_version and payload_digest as well as account/resource/destination/currency/action. The trusted host must verify the digest against the actual immutable payload bytes; it must not accept a caller-supplied digest as truth. The request/grant schemas define validated inputs; helper guards independently reject missing/mismatched dimensions. No request or grant object is an authority issuer.
